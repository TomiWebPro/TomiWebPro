Tomi Respects Your Privacy

If you find any of the content violating your right to privacy, wrong, or misleading, please file a takedown request here.

File a Request

Whether you are requesting the removal of personal information, a factual correction to a project log or biographical note, or a formal copyright/DMCA takedown notice, requests are processed promptly, respectfully, and directly:

Please Include the Following in Your Message:

  1. Target URL: The exact page on tomiweb.pro (e.g., https://tomiweb.pro/public/... or https://tomiweb.pro/private/...).
  2. Nature of Request: Right to privacy / personal redaction, factual error or misleading statement, or copyright/DMCA notice.
  3. Specific Details: The exact text, image, or reference in question and the reason for removal or revision.
  4. Desired Resolution: Complete takedown, redaction, or replacement with corrected text.

Editorial & Privacy Principles

Direct & Responsive:

There are no bureaucratic ticket queues or automated denials. Tomi reviews submissions personally and makes requested corrections or removals swiftly upon verification.

Zero Friction:

If you are mentioned anywhere on this site and would prefer not to be, let Tomi know and your mention will be removed or pseudonymized immediately without argument.

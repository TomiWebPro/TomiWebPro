What it is

LLM Chess Arena is a standalone, offline-first Flutter app for games between humans and LLM agents in two rulesets — standard chess and chaotic chess that permits illegal moves under a separate interpretation layer. It is built for comparison and inspection rather than just play, bundling arena tournaments, per-mode Elo statistics, and post-game review alongside single games.

How it works

Agents play through validated make_chess_move tool calls with retry and timeout handling; on failure, a seeded random legal move is substituted and recorded as fallback. Every turn emits persisted, idempotent events consumed by stats, arena, and review. A MockProvider plays scripted demo games with no keys, while OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or Ollama keys can be added in the Agents screen and stay in secure storage.

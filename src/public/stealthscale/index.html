Problem & Motivation

During travels to China following graduation from high school, accessing international computational resources, academic databases, and personal home servers was severely impaired by state-level firewall filters and Deep Packet Inspection (DPI).

While tools like Tailscale and WireGuard provide incredible peer-to-peer connectivity under normal network conditions, their handshake signatures, UDP packet sizes, and unpadded crypto headers are trivial for modern DPI systems to detect and selectively drop or throttle.

Rather than relying on closed, commercial, or easily blockable consumer VPNs, Tomi engineered Stealthscale: combining the decentralized mesh topology of Tailscale/Headscale with custom transport obfuscation layers that masquerade as standard web traffic.

Key Architectural Features

• True TLS 1.3 Handshake Mimicry: Sessions negotiate valid cryptographic handshakes mimicking modern browser TLS fingerprints (JA3/JA4 and cipher suite ordering), eliminating synthetic handshake anomalies.

• Entropy & Padding Shaping: Injects variable padding and randomized timing jitter to match real web browsing traffic distributions, bypassing statistical entropy analyzers.

• Self-Hosted Coordination Server: Engineered around Headscale primitives, ensuring zero reliance on third-party SaaS control planes.

Genesis & Concept

After finishing the International Baccalaureate examinations in May 2026, Tomi reflected on his experiences using online 1-on-1 tutoring platforms like Preply. While human tutors provide personalized guidance, they are costly and difficult to scale across demanding university STEM subjects. StudyKing was started in March 2026 during IB exam preparation to replace that dependency.

StudyKing addresses this by exploring how modern LLMs can deliver interactive, diagnostic feedback at zero marginal cost. Developed continuously via 24/7 AI coding agents (initially DeepSeek) with daily commits, the autonomous multimodal system ingests PDFs, lecture audio and video, slides, photos, and prompts — converting them into structured presentations, explanations, and progress-tracked quizzes tailored specifically for university-level engineering and computer science curricula.

Current Status

StudyKing is under active development as Tomi progresses through his B.Sc. in Information Engineering at the Technical University of Munich. The core focus is automated syllabus parsing, mathematical LaTeX question synthesis, and tracking student concept mastery graphs.

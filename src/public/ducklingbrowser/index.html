What it is

Duckling Browser is an anti-detect browser on stock Chromium with per-profile fingerprint isolation: unlimited profiles with their own cookies, extensions, and data, per-profile proxies and VPN configs, and spoofing aimed at Cloudflare, reCAPTCHA, and fingerprinting services — plus a local REST API and MCP server for automation.

Fork story

A community fork of Donut Browser that removes all paid and pro subscription gating, so every feature ships free and unlocked by default. Ships for macOS, Windows, and Linux, with zero telemetry.

Genesis & Concept

After finishing the International Baccalaureate examinations in May 2026, I reflected on my experiences using online 1-on-1 language and subject tutoring platforms like Preply. While human tutors provide personalized guidance, they are costly and difficult to scale across demanding university STEM subjects.

I asked myself: can modern LLMs provide that exact interactive, responsive feedback loop at zero marginal cost? StudyKing was created to act as an autonomous learning companion tailored specifically for university-level engineering and computer science curricula.

Key Capabilities

[COURSE SYLLABUS / TOPIC GOAL] (e.g. Signals & Systems, Linear Algebra)
               |
               v
[CURRICULUM DECOMPOSITION ENGINE]
  - Breaks high-level course modules into micro-competencies
  - Estimates prerequisite graphs & optimal study paths
               |
               v
[ADAPTIVE PRACTICE & EVALUATION]
  - Dynamically synthesizes practice problems tailored to current skill level
  - Evaluates user responses with step-by-step diagnostic feedback
               |
               v
[KNOWLEDGE RETENTION ENGINE]
  - Spaced repetition scheduling based on error frequency
  - Generates synthetic quizzes right before key examination milestones
Current Status

StudyKing is under active development as I progress through my B.Sc. in Information Engineering at the Technical University of Munich. The core focus is currently on automated syllabus parsing, mathematical LaTeX question synthesis, and tracking student concept mastery graphs.
